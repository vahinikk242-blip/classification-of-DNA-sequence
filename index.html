3. Interpretation & Discussion

A. Genomics Application

Machine learning classifiers in genomics are extensively used for metagenomic binning and pathogen identification. By analyzing motifs and GC content, ML models can rapidly classify unknown sequencing reads to specific taxonomies, aiding in outbreak tracking or microbiome profiling.

B. Limitations of AI/ML

Out-of-Distribution Vulnerability: AI models rely heavily on the distribution of their training data. If presented with a highly mutated sequence or an entirely novel organism, the model cannot flag it as "unknown" by default; it will force a prediction into existing classes, leading to confident but incorrect classifications.
